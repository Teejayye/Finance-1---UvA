# Finance-1---UvA
Link: **https://teejayye.github.io/Finance-1---UvA/**

A study site for **Finance 1 for Business - 6012B0422Y** (UvA).

Each week has:

- a summary, formulas (marked as on the formula sheet or learn by heart) and a searchable glossary;
- practice questions that explain every answer option;
- a weekly quiz and the homework, step by step.

Exam practice:

- **Past papers**: Midterm 2024 and Midterm 2019, in the original wording. Check each answer as you go, or finish the paper and get a grade.
- **Mock midterms A, B and C**: 60 new exam-level questions on weeks 1–3. The wrong options come from common calculation mistakes, and the correct answers are spread evenly over A–D.

Good to know:

- **Progress is saved per browser** (localStorage): practice results, best scores and homework answers. Friends each have their own progress. Clearing browser data resets it.
- **Exam dates** and a countdown are on the home page.
- **Light/dark mode**: use the button at the top right.
